## Numerical synthesis that was executed

`notebooks/actual_20261022/sintesis_20261022.py` was executed from the published derived CSV subset. It checks SHA-256 input hashes, finite profile geometry, increasing distance, positive area, normalized trapezoidal χ, 24 calibration labels and 18 distinct physical calibration points. It averages K by distinct physical crossing before calculating times and keeps the two MIS separate.

The synthesis reproduces the current reference time and near-knickpoint rate tables within the explicit tolerances in `verificar_referencia`. Reloca uses C2 with a 5e lower calibration and alternative 5c/5e upper donors. Its anchor residuals are exported, not hidden. K transfer to northern and southern receptors, adopted origins and the changed-area Curanilahue geometry remain explicit. The new rates use up to 2 km immediately downstream of each KP; they do not use the B–Pₜ calibration interval. This is parity with a saved derived-data summary, not an independent test of terrace ages, DEM accuracy, manual geometry or tectonic causation.

## Static checks and remaining limits

The validation report records Jupyter-schema checks, Python compilation, stripped-output checks, personal-path/credential scanning and input-file hashes. Supporting JavaScript is checked separately for syntax. Schema and syntax checks do not establish that every research stage runs successfully with an external input archive.

01–06 were not rerun. Full reproduction from the DEM still needs compatible terrace catalogues, manual B/KP decisions, fault layers, polygons, prior products/manifests and the correct TopoAnalysis installation. Moving a manifest or changing a dependency does not automatically repair its path or hash chain. Optional browser links and PDF font availability may need platform-specific configuration. The minimum synthesis environment is recorded separately from an unvalidated full research environment.

Chronological correlations, current versus former substrate cover, Reloca anchor consistency, the adopted drainage-area scenario, vertical datum and joint uncertainty propagation remain scientific limitations. Publishing source and matching derived tables does not remove them. Future changes to these assumptions require a new dated run and new reference tables.
